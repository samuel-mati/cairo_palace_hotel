Restaurant

A kitchen built around two cuisines

Somali grill and a dedicated Indian kitchen, running side by side. The sample below reflects what regularly appears on the menu; dishes rotate with what is fresh.

Full board packages are available for guests staying more than a night or two, covering breakfast, lunch, and dinner at a fixed daily rate. Ask the front desk for current full board pricing when you book your room.

Outside of the room, the restaurant also serves walk in guests and hosts catering for conferences and events held on the grounds.
