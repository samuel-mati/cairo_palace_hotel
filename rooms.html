Rooms and rates

Three room types, one standard of security

Every room comes with high speed Wi-Fi, 24 hour room service, and access to the same guarded grounds. Rates below are indicative starting prices; call the front desk to confirm current pricing and availability for your dates.

Room type 01

Single

From KES 3,500 per night

Right sized for one traveller, fully equipped, and priced for people moving through Kakuma on their own, whether for a night or an extended posting.

  • One bed, private bathroom
  • High speed Wi-Fi
  • 24 hour room service
  • Writing desk
  • Fan and mosquito netting
  • Daily housekeeping
Rate shown is per night, room only. Full board and long stay rates available on request.
Room type 02

Comfort Double

From KES 5,000 per night

Built for couples or two colleagues sharing, with a private balcony and enough room to spread out after a long drive up the highway.

  • Double bed, private balcony
  • Deluxe bath amenities
  • High speed Wi-Fi
  • 24 hour room service
  • Seating area
  • Daily housekeeping
Rate shown is per night, room only. Ask about weekend rates that include breakfast.
Room type 03

VIP Suite

From KES 7,000 per night

The largest room on the property. Guests consistently describe it as spacious, clean, and cool, with air conditioning that holds up against Turkana's heat.

  • Largest floor plan on site
  • Private balcony
  • Air conditioning
  • Deluxe bath amenities
  • High speed Wi-Fi
  • 24 hour room service
Rate shown is per night, room only. Full board packages available for extended stays.
