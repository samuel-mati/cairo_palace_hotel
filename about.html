About the hotel

Built for the people who actually travel through Kakuma

Not a resort, and not trying to be one. Cairo Palace is a working hotel for a working town, run by people who understand exactly who walks through the gate.

A hotel shaped by its location

Kakuma sits on the Kakuma to Lokichogio highway in Turkana County, close to the South Sudan border and to one of the largest refugee camps in East Africa. It draws a steady flow of humanitarian staff, government officials, traders, and families, none of whom are looking for a beach holiday. They need somewhere secure to sleep, eat well, and hold a meeting the next morning.

Cairo Palace Hotel was built around that reality. The grounds sit behind a guarded gate, the restaurant runs long hours because guests arrive at every hour, and the rooms are laid out for people who will use them as a base rather than a backdrop.

Guests often mention the grounds first: lawns and shade trees kept green through a hot, dry climate, a small but genuine change of pace after a long stretch on the highway.

What guests can count on

Four things that stay consistent no matter which room you book.

Security first

A manned gate, enclosed perimeter, and staff on the property overnight, every night of the year.

Straight answers on price

Rates are quoted plainly over the phone before you travel, with no surprise charges at checkout.

A kitchen that adapts

Somali grill, Indian dishes, and full board packages, built around who is actually staying that week.

Room to work

Conference space and event grounds used regularly for training sessions and organisational meetings.

Where you will actually be

The hotel sits on the Kakuma to Lokichogio highway, roughly 1.4 km from Kakuma town centre and close to Shepherds Hospital. Lodwar, the nearest airport, is about 120 km away on a road guests consistently describe as smooth and quiet.

Turkana's climate is hot and dry for most of the year, which is part of why the hotel puts real effort into its grounds. Shade, watered lawns, and a covered gathering area make a difference guests notice within the first few minutes of arriving.
