Kakuma is not a resort town. It sits on a frontier route near the South Sudan border, and Cairo Palace was built for the people who actually pass through it: arriving tired, staying for work, needing somewhere that feels safe the moment the gate closes behind them.

1.4 km from Kakuma town
On the Kakuma to Lokichogio highway, close to town without sitting in the middle of it.
Gated and guarded, day and night
A staffed security gate and enclosed grounds kept green in a hot, dry landscape.
Open every hour, every day
Front desk, restaurant, and room service run around the clock, seven days a week.
